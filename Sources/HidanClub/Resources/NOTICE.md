# Hidan Club

This prototype includes original textual practice prompts and a procedurally generated metronome groove. No third-party dance videos, commercial music, model weights, or research motion datasets are bundled.

The Hidan Club dancer logo was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The full-resolution PNG and standard macOS ICNS representations are stored in Brand/. Design direction and the final prompt are documented in docs/BRAND.md in the source project.
